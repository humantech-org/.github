# HumanTech

**Software designed around people.**

Humantech designs and builds applications around the people who use them: calm, private and minimal tools that
respect your attention and your data, and help with what you care about. It's the studio of Massimo Brivio, an
engineer from ETH Zürich, based in Switzerland.

## How we build

1. **Start from one person.** Every app solves a real problem for someone we can empathize with.
2. **Calm by design.** No feeds, no ads, no nagging.
3. **Yours to keep.** Your data stays private. Where it makes sense, you can run our apps on your own server for free.
